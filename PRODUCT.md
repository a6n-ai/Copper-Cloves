# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Three distinct audiences sharing one platform:

- **Members**: Urban wellness seekers in India booking classes, buying packages, and ordering from the café. On their phone, between work and a session, wanting a frictionless path to class. They chose this studio over a gym chain deliberately.
- **Public visitors**: Potential members discovering the studio via word-of-mouth or search. They need to feel the atmosphere before committing. Warmth and credibility convert them.
- **Staff (admin/instructor)**: Studio team managing schedules, check-ins, and operations. Functionality over aesthetics; they are power users who know the tool.

## Product Purpose

The Studio by Copper + Cloves is a wellness studio in India that runs fitness classes, a plant-based café, and a boutique. The platform has two layers: a brand-facing public site that markets the studio and converts visitors to members, and a member/ops layer (portals) that handles bookings, packages, check-ins, and studio management. Success looks like: a visitor lands, feels the warmth, signs up; a member books without friction; a class fills.

## Brand Personality

Warm, grounded, communal. Tagline: "Your home away from home." Voice is confident but never corporate, personal but never precious. The studio is a sanctuary that takes wellness seriously without taking itself too seriously.

## Anti-references

- **Generic SaaS**: gradient heroes, blue CTA buttons, metric dashboards with gradient accents, identical card grids.
- **Big-box gym**: dark + neon, aggressive hyper-masculine type, stock photography of veins and weights.
- **Instagram wellness**: pastel gradient overload, cursive script everywhere, flat "aesthetic" over substance.
- **Sterile spa/clinic**: all-white, sans-serif only, cold silence. Wellness without warmth.

## Design Principles

1. **Real over polished**: Warmth comes from texture, imperfection, and human presence. Avoid surfaces that look generated.
2. **Community, not transaction**: Even a booking confirmation should feel like a studio touchpoint, not a receipt.
3. **Nature-rooted palette**: Sage, sand, cream, terracotta are not decorative choices; they are the brand. Every surface defers to them.
4. **Motion earns its place**: Video and animation exist to reveal character, not fill silence. Calm pages breathe.
5. **Functional portals, branded shell**: Admin and member portal internals are product-register (function-first), but the navigation and shell keep the brand's warmth. The line between brand and product is the navigation, not a hard wall.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG AA minimum. All interactive elements keyboard-navigable. Contrast ratios meet AA for body text and interactive controls. Reduced motion: no hard requirement, but large-scale animations (hero video crossfades, scroll-triggered sections) should respect `prefers-reduced-motion` where feasible.
