# Copper & Cloves — Platform Feature Overview

*The Studio by Copper + Cloves — an all-in-one platform for wellness & fitness studios: class booking, packages, café ordering, boutique retail, member CRM, and studio operations.*

This document lists the capabilities available on the platform. It is organised by who uses each feature.

---

## 1. For Members (the people who attend your studio)

### Booking & Classes
- Browse the full class schedule by day, discipline, and instructor.
- Book a class in a few taps; instant confirmation.
- Bring guests — add extra attendees or invite other members to the same booking.
- View upcoming and past bookings in one place.
- Cancel a booking, with clear cancellation policy and eligibility shown up front.
- Request a refund on eligible cancellations; track the refund status end-to-end.
- Booking detail page shows class status and payment status at a glance.

### Passes & Packages
- Buy class passes and studio passes online.
- Time-bound special offers and promotional pricing.
- Coupon / promo code support, including stackable offers.
- Track remaining credits and pass validity automatically.
- Café discount applied automatically to pass holders at checkout.

### Café & Boutique
- Order from the studio café menu — standalone or alongside a class booking.
- Live price quote showing pass discounts and coupons before you pay.
- Shop the studio boutique; browse products and check out online.
- Monthly meal subscription plans with a remaining-meals balance.

### Payments
- Secure online payments via Razorpay (cards, UPI, and more) in INR.
- Reliable fulfilment — if a payment succeeds but the app hiccups, the platform automatically reconciles and delivers what was paid for.
- Full payment history.

### Profile & Engagement
- Personal profile with avatar upload, contact details, and health questionnaire.
- Streak tracking and attendance stats.
- Badges and a path-to-mastery progression system to keep members motivated.
- Support tickets (e.g. pause a subscription) raised directly from the app.
- Class reminder emails ~1 hour before each session.
- Signed digital liability waivers.

---

## 2. For Studio Admins (your team running the studio)

### Dashboard & Analytics
- Revenue, class, and member overview at a glance.
- Today's classes with live check-in status.
- Members whose packages are expiring soon (≤14 days).
- Per-class booking volume and discipline split.
- Per-instructor performance: classes, check-ins, ratings.
- Member-of-the-month, top classes, no-show and late-arrival counts, inactive members.
- Full finance ledger across packages and booking checkouts.

### Schedule & Class Management
- Create and edit class schedules and class types.
- Automatic edit-locking once a class is completed or past-due (protects historical records).
- Roster stays editable (add member, check-in, mark no-show) even on locked classes.

### Member Management
- Full member list with credit and package assignment.
- Typeahead member search by name or email.
- Assign passes with catalog pricing in a single guided step.
- Credit transaction history.
- Manage member support tickets (e.g. subscription pauses).

### Payments & Finance
- Record offline / walk-in payments: cash, card machine (Pine Labs), direct UPI, etc.
- Attach proof-of-payment images.
- Unified payment ledger across every method — online and offline.
- Reconcile paid-but-stuck orders; import missing payments.
- Export finance reports.

### Café, Boutique & CRM
- Café menu management and order view.
- Boutique product management and orders.
- Built-in CRM: email + WhatsApp templates, triggers, and message log.
- Automated notifications on package purchase (email + WhatsApp).

### Badges & Gamification
- Define custom badges and path-to-mastery milestones.
- Auto-award badges at attendance thresholds; allocate custom badges to members.

### Instructor Payouts
- Configurable tiered payout rate card (with GST handling).
- Per-instructor payout calculation per period.
- Per-attendee ledger that reconciles to payable units.
- Overrides and "mark paid" with a frozen snapshot for audit.

---

## 3. For Instructors

- Personal portal with today's and this week's classes.
- Member check-in from the class roster.
- Instructor self check-in window.
- Unpaid / payment-pending attendees clearly flagged, with a reminder option.
- Payout visibility per period.

---

## 4. For Partners (outside brands renting your studio)

*For studios that host third-party operators (e.g. a guest fitness brand) running their own classes on your premises.*

- Dedicated partner portal with their own branded login.
- Scoped only to their own classes and members.
- Week/month calendar with rosters.
- Confirm or reject pending bookings for their classes.
- Manage their own brand (name, logo, description) and login details.
- Dashboard with today's classes and stats.

---

## 5. Public Website (converting visitors to members)

- Marketing pages: home, classes, café, rental, shop, founder story, meal subscription, policy & terms.
- Space-rental enquiry form.
- Meal-subscription waitlist.
- Boutique storefront open to the public.
- Warm, editorial brand design — built to feel like a real studio, not a generic app.

---

## 6. Platform & Reliability

- **Single unified login** for every role (member, instructor, partner, admin) — one email, correct portal.
- **Multi-channel notifications**: email (with automatic fallback provider) and WhatsApp.
- **Automated background jobs**: class reminders, no-show reconciliation, payment recovery, seat-release for unpaid holds.
- **Mobile-first**: fully responsive across phone, tablet, and desktop.
- **Dark mode** support.
- **Secure by design**: role-based access, server-side payment verification, signed webhooks.
- **Accessibility**: WCAG AA baseline, keyboard navigation, reduced-motion support.

---

*Feature set as of July 2026. Individual modules can be enabled or tailored per studio.*
