# Design System: The Studio by Copper + Cloves

## 1. Overview

**Creative North Star: "The Copper & Clay Studio"**

This is a design system built like a handcrafted object, not a software product. Every surface should feel like it was shaped by someone who cared, not generated by a system that scaled. The palette draws from raw materials: fired terracotta, sage-green herbs, warm sand, and aged cream. The typography pairs Playfair Display's editorial authority with Montserrat's clean utility — editorial weight for moments of intention, functional weight for the work of booking and navigating.

The system is fundamentally warm-register. It rejects the cold precision of SaaS and the aggressive maximalism of a gym brand. A visitor should feel like they've walked into a Bangalore studio that smells like eucalyptus and fresh coffee, not landed on a conversion-optimized landing page. Screens breathe. Space is generous. Color is used deliberately, not sprinkled.

The brand surface (public marketing pages) is the voice. The product surface (portals and admin) is the hands. Both share the same palette and type system; the portal surfaces are simply quieter, putting more white space to work and reducing decorative moves entirely.

**Key Characteristics:**
- Warm, earthy palette anchored by sage and terracotta — neither is decorative, both are structural
- Serif-led display hierarchy with Playfair Display; Montserrat carries all functional UI text
- Generous negative space; sections breathe rather than stack tightly
- Photography is the primary decorative layer — real food, real instructors, real space
- Lifted surface feedback on interaction; flat by default

## 2. Colors: The Earth & Herb Palette

Four brand colors plus a neutral system. No color exists purely for decoration — each has a structural role.

### Primary
- **Sage** (`#8f9779`): The anchor color. Used for primary actions (buttons, CTAs), the site footer, active navigation states, and progress indicators. Muted green-grey; reads as grounded and earthy without performing "wellness" too loudly.

### Secondary
- **Terracotta** (`#c17856`): The warmth accent. Used for secondary emphasis, icon color in energy-forward contexts (café, food), hover states on outline elements, and alert/badge highlights. Never used on the same surface as sage unless they're in separate zones.

### Neutral
- **Cream** (`#f5f2ea`): Primary page background. All marketing surfaces sit on cream. Never pure white; cream reads as warm and physical.
- **Sand** (`#e8e4d9`): Secondary surface. Muted section backgrounds, chip/badge backgrounds, secondary button fill. Distinct from cream but in the same family.
- **Charcoal** (`#333333`): All body copy. Not black — charcoal is warm dark that holds against cream.
- **Warm Border** (`#e5e4dc`): All dividers, card borders, input strokes.
- **Muted Surface** (`#f4f3ec`): Hover states on cards and list items; section background alternative to cream.
- **Muted Text** (`#6b6b6b`): Secondary text, metadata, helper copy.
- **White-warm** (`#fafaf8`): Card and panel backgrounds. Slightly warmer than `#ffffff` — never use pure `#ffffff` or `#000000`.

### Named Rules
**The Two-Voice Rule.** Sage and terracotta never share equal visual weight on the same surface. One leads; the other accents at ≤30% of the dominant. If sage owns the CTA button, terracotta can appear as an icon color or badge. Never co-equal.

**The No-Pure-White Rule.** All surfaces use `#fafaf8` or warmer. Pure white (`#ffffff`) is prohibited on large surfaces. On cream backgrounds, cards read as white-warm but are never clinically white.

## 3. Typography

**Display Font:** Playfair Display, Georgia, serif
**Body Font:** Montserrat, system-ui, sans-serif

**Character:** Playfair Display carries editorial authority — used for moments of intention (section headings, hero copy, product names). Montserrat is the functional workhorse: navigation, body copy, labels, all UI text inside portals. The pairing is a deliberate high-contrast split: the display font has personality and weight; the body font is neutral and readable. They do not overlap.

### Hierarchy
- **Display** (700, `clamp(2.5rem, 7vw, 4.5rem)`, line-height 1.1): Section heroes, page headings, major marketing moments. Italic cuts in Playfair (`font-style: italic`) used sparingly for single-word emphasis (e.g. *Host* Your Event). Never used for body copy.
- **Headline** (600, `clamp(1.75rem, 4vw, 2.5rem)`, line-height 1.2): Sub-section headings, card titles in marketing contexts, instructor names in feature contexts.
- **Title** (500, 1.25rem, line-height 1.4): Card headings inside portals, dialog titles, sidebar section labels.
- **Body** (400, 1rem, line-height 1.7): All prose. Max line length 65-75ch on marketing pages; portal copy can go tighter. Montserrat only.
- **Label** (600, 0.75rem, letter-spacing 0.08em, ALL-CAPS optional): Navigation links, chip/badge text, metadata, status indicators. If uppercase, cap at 0.75rem — any larger at all-caps reads as shouting.

### Named Rules
**The Strict Handoff Rule.** Playfair Display is forbidden in portal UI text (form labels, table cells, nav items, button copy). It crosses the register boundary. Montserrat handles all UI surfaces; Playfair handles all brand surfaces.

**The Italic Sparingly Rule.** Italic Playfair is a single-use emphasis tool per section. One italic word in a heading is voice; two italic headings per screen is affectation.

## 4. Elevation

This system is flat at rest and responds on interaction. Surfaces sit on the page without shadows; depth is expressed through tonal layering (cream → sand → white-warm) rather than shadow casting. Cards at rest have only a warm border (`1px solid #e5e4dc`) and no shadow.

On hover and focus, interactive surfaces lift with a soft, diffuse shadow. The transition communicates "this responds to you" — tactile feedback consistent with the handcrafted feel of the system.

### Shadow Vocabulary
- **Lifted** (`box-shadow: 0 4px 24px rgba(51, 51, 51, 0.08)`): Applied on card hover, modal containers, active dropdown menus. Warm-tinted shadow (references charcoal, not pure black) so it doesn't look cold.
- **Deep** (`box-shadow: 0 8px 48px rgba(51, 51, 51, 0.14)`): Hero image containers, full-bleed overlapping image sections only. Not used on repeatable UI components.
- **Focus ring** (`box-shadow: 0 0 0 2px #8f9779`): Sage-colored focus ring on all interactive elements. Never blue.

### Named Rules
**The Flat-By-Default Rule.** A card at rest is flat. Shadow appears only as a response to hover or elevation state. If a card already has a shadow in its default state, it has no room to respond to interaction and the system has no depth grammar. Forbidden: `shadow-2xl` on resting cards.

## 5. Components

### Buttons
- **Shape:** Gently rounded (8px radius, `rounded-md`). Not pill, not square.
- **Primary:** Sage background (`#8f9779`), white-warm text, 12px 24px padding. Transitions to darker sage (`#7A8B7C`) on hover with a `translateY(-1px)` lift.
- **Secondary:** Sand background (`#e8e4d9`), charcoal text. Used for adjacent non-primary actions.
- **Ghost:** Transparent, charcoal text, subtle border on hover. Used in portal for non-destructive secondary actions.
- **Hover / Focus:** `transition: background 200ms ease-out, transform 150ms ease-out`. Sage focus ring (2px, `box-shadow: 0 0 0 2px #8f9779`). No outline-style focus.

### Cards / Containers
- **Corner Style:** 16px radius (`rounded-2xl`) on marketing cards; 12px (`rounded-lg`) on portal UI cards. Never pill-shaped containers.
- **Background:** White-warm (`#fafaf8`) on cream backgrounds; sand (`#e8e4d9`) for muted/secondary containers.
- **Shadow Strategy:** Flat at rest (border only). Lifted (`0 4px 24px rgba(51,51,51,0.08)`) on hover. See Elevation.
- **Border:** 1px warm border (`#e5e4dc`). Increases opacity to `#c8c6be` on hover.
- **Internal Padding:** 32px (`p-8`) on marketing feature cards; 20px (`p-5`) on portal compact cards.

### Inputs / Fields
- **Style:** 1px warm-border stroke, white-warm background, 8px radius.
- **Focus:** Border shifts to sage (`#8f9779`); sage focus ring (`box-shadow: 0 0 0 2px rgba(143, 151, 121, 0.3)`). No blue focus.
- **Error:** Destructive red (`hsl(8 62% 60%)`) border + icon, no background fill change.
- **Disabled:** 50% opacity on the whole field. No interaction affordance.

### Navigation
- **Default (overlay):** Transparent background, white logo and links — sits on top of the hero video.
- **Scrolled / Default variant:** Cream background (`#f5f2ea`), charcoal text links, sage CTA button.
- **Mobile:** Hamburger collapses to a full-screen sheet with centered links, large Playfair display type. Not a sidebar.
- **Active link:** Underline in sage; no bold weight shift (weight shift on a sans link at small size reads as layout jitter).

### Video Hero Panels
Three equal-width panels, each a full-height video or image crossfade. Text label (`font-script`, Playfair italic, white/90) anchored to the bottom-center of each panel. Gradient overlay `from-black/50 via-transparent to-transparent` on each panel bottom. This is the site's signature component — do not collapse it to a single image or a traditional hero.

### Pricing Cards (Horizontal Carousel)
Horizontal scroll carousel of package cards. Featured card (`featured: true`) renders with sage background and white text. All others: white-warm background, charcoal text, warm border. Benefits list uses Check icons in sage. Scroll controlled by prev/next arrow buttons. Tabs toggle between "Class Pass" and "Studio Pass" tier groups.

## 6. Do's and Don'ts

### Do:
- **Do** use Playfair Display for all section headings, product names, and brand moments. Switch to Montserrat at the component/UI layer boundary.
- **Do** use cream (`#f5f2ea`) as the primary page background, not white. The warmth is the brand.
- **Do** let photography carry the decorative load. Real images of food, instructors, and the studio space are the primary texture.
- **Do** vary section heading alignment, size, and structure as you move down the landing page. Some headings centered, some left-aligned, some italic-only without a subhead below.
- **Do** use sage for primary CTAs consistently. One color owns the action, every time.
- **Do** add `@media (prefers-reduced-motion: reduce)` guards on all keyframe animations, especially the hero video `animateFloatAndZoom` and crossfade transitions.
- **Do** use terracotta as a warmth accent in café and food contexts; use sage as the studio/movement accent. Let context guide which appears.
- **Do** keep portal milestone/gamification colors within the brand palette (sage and terracotta tones) rather than defaulting to Tailwind amber or yellow.
- **Do** apply sage as the focus ring color on all interactive elements (buttons, inputs, links).

### Don't:
- **Don't** use identical card grids: icon above heading above text, three across, same size. Prohibited on any surface. Restructure as editorial blocks, asymmetric layouts, or image-led sections.
- **Don't** use `backdrop-blur` for decorative floating badges. Glassmorphism used decoratively is prohibited. If a stat or label needs to sit on an image, use a solid white-warm panel with warm border.
- **Don't** add large soft-blur decorative circles (`rounded-full blur-3xl`) to section backgrounds as texture. This move was used on 3 consecutive sections; it signals a template, not a design. Use tonal background color shifts or photography instead.
- **Don't** assign all testimonials 5 stars. Uniform 5-star ratings signal fabrication. Either remove the rating system or use realistic variation.
- **Don't** use pure `#ffffff` or `#000000` on any surface. All backgrounds are cream-shifted; all text is charcoal.
- **Don't** use Playfair Display for portal UI labels, table cells, navigation links, or form copy. It crosses the register boundary.
- **Don't** load Bricolage Grotesque (or any unused font family) in the Google Fonts import. Every unused family costs 20-40KB on every page load.
- **Don't** make the design look like: a generic SaaS landing page (gradient heroes, blue CTAs, metric tiles), a big-box gym (neon, aggressive type, stock muscle photography), Instagram wellness (pastel gradients, script overload), or a sterile clinic (all-white, cold minimalism, sans-serif only).
- **Don't** use `shadow-2xl` on resting card states. Shadows are earned by interaction, not applied at rest.
- **Don't** use Tailwind amber or yellow utilities (`amber-600`, `yellow-600`) for portal gamification milestones. Use brand palette: sage tones for early milestones, terracotta tones for peak achievements.
